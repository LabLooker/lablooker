Design notes

  • 4px left chip — only on Out of Range (brick rose) and Suboptimal (amber) rows
  • Right side: colored pill for flagged rows, muted "In Range" text for good rows, blank for no-range rows
  • Whole row is clickable (cursor: pointer, hover highlight)
  • Chart icon visible always on mobile, fades in on hover on desktop
  • ℹ️ button next to Suboptimal pill — tap to toggle tooltip
  • Dividers separate "Needs attention" from "In range" sections
  • No range rows: no chip, no status label — just the value and chart icon
