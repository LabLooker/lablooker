## Platform Comparison Notes

### Ulta Lab Tests
- Uses Quest Diagnostics exclusively
- No monthly fee; pay per test
- Prices generally 10-30% lower than Walk-In Lab for Quest-routed tests
- All 50 states covered for Quest (excluding NY, NJ, RI for some tests)
- Results delivered to patient portal
- Test catalog: extensive (comparable to Quest's full menu)

### Walk-In Lab
- Uses both Quest Diagnostics AND LabCorp
- 1,251+ individual tests and panels
- LabCorp network: blacklisted states MA, NY, NJ, RI, MD
- Quest network: blacklisted states NY, NJ, RI
- Prices generally slightly higher than Ulta but more network options
- Frequent promotional pricing (15-20% codes available)
- Good selection of specialty panels
